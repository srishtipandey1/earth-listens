# 90-second demo: Earth Listens

The demo begins in silence, then opens with the newly available Arctic September sea-ice scene. The NSIDC source data is real and unmodified; no sample values are used.

| Time | Action and narration |
|---|---|
| 0:00-0:06 | **Silence.** Show the page without touching Play. “Earth has a story in its data. We begin with no sound until you choose to listen.” |
| 0:06-0:17 | Choose **Arctic sea ice — September extent** and point to About the data. “This is the Northern Hemisphere September record from the NSIDC Sea Ice Index, with one observed value for every year from 1979 through 2026. The circle's area follows the measured extent.” |
| 0:17-0:30 | Use **Hear 1979**, then **Hear the record low (2012)**. “The broken ring marks this series' 1981-2010 mean, 6.413333 million square kilometers. 1979 is 7.05; the record low in 2012 is 3.57.” |
| 0:30-0:42 | Enable **Compare two years**, set the right year to 1979, and press **Play**. “The two periods appear side by side and play in the left and right ears. The much lower 2012 extent adds tension, brightness, and a more unsettled sound.” |
| 0:42-0:51 | Point to the data note while playback is paused. “The NSIDC Version 4 guide documents an input-source switch: NSIDC-0051 through 2024, then AMSR2/NSIDC-0803 from 2025. It attributes that change to expected loss of SSMIS access and observed data degradation.” |
| 0:51-1:05 | Set a birth year available in the active scene, choose **Quick**, and press **Play my Earth song**. “The same year-by-year mapping follows this personal timeline to the latest observation. The year and value stay in view, with spoken progress every five years.” |
| 1:05-1:15 | Restart with **Save my song** and let the short segment reach the latest year. “The browser records the instrument output—not microphone audio—and downloads the completed file in the format shown beside the control.” |
| 1:15-1:23 | Focus the year scrubber and use ArrowLeft/ArrowRight, press Space to play/pause, and press M to mute/unmute. “The controls work by keyboard, and the live announcement describes the selected year and extent.” |
| 1:23-1:30 | Select **Global temperature anomaly**, then open **About the data** or **How we used AI**. “The warming stripes, source citations, processing notes, and remaining limits are all visible.” |

## Before presenting

- Start with a fresh page load and leave audio silent until the deliberate Play action.
- For the saved-song segment, choose a year near the latest observation so the recording completes within the demo; use the format named in the interface.
- Confirm the raw September extent file remains the supplied 1979-2026 sequence and the source transition note matches the current NSIDC guide.
- Confirm NASA attribution and current dataset terms, and check event AI-use/submission requirements.
- If either dataset fails to load, stop the demo and explain the error; do not substitute a sample.
