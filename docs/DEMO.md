# 90-second demo: Earth Listens

**Important:** this build does not have the required Arctic sea-ice data. The scene is disabled, so do not claim to play it. The script starts in silence as requested, discloses that limit, and demonstrates the one real, playable scene instead.

| Time | Action and narration |
|---|---|
| 0:00-0:06 | **Silence.** Show the page without touching Play. “Earth has a story in its data. We begin with no sound until you choose to listen.” |
| 0:06-0:15 | Point to the scene list. “Arctic sea ice is our intended opening, but its official source could not be verified in this build, so it is disabled rather than filled with invented data. The real series we can demonstrate is NASA GISS global temperature.” |
| 0:15-0:29 | Move the year slider to 2024. “Each point is NASA GISS's annual global anomaly relative to 1951-1980. In 2024 the bundled record is +1.28 degrees Celsius.” |
| 0:29-0:43 | Press **Play**. “The melody stays on a calm pentatonic path. Distance from the baseline adds brightness and a near-note tension; faster annual change adds more pulses and a soft noise layer.” |
| 0:43-0:59 | Pause, enable **Compare periods**, set the comparison slider near 1950, and press **Play**. “The two selected periods occupy left and right stereo positions. The chart helps locate them, but the live announcement also describes the year and value.” |
| 0:59-1:12 | Use ArrowLeft/ArrowRight on the year slider, press Space to pause, and press M to mute/unmute. “The controls work by keyboard, the current observation is announced, and mute stays visible.” |
| 1:12-1:23 | Open **About the data**, then **How we used AI**. “The source, access date, processing, and use guidance are shown here. Only this temperature scene is currently bundled.” |
| 1:23-1:30 | Stop playback. “Next we need human listening and license review, and verified access to the Arctic and vegetation data before the complete three-scene demo.” |

## Before presenting

- Start with a fresh page load and leave audio silent until the deliberate Play action.
- If data fails to load, stop the demo and explain the error; do not substitute a sample.
- Confirm NASA attribution and current dataset terms, and check event AI-use/submission requirements.
